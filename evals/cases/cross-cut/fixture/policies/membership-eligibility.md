# Membership eligibility policy

Set by the committee; last changed 2025-05-03. Depends on:
[accounts](../reference/accounts.md).

Toolshed is the residents' association's, run for the people who live in
the area it serves and insured on that basis. Who may join follows from
that: the insurer covers tools lent to residents, the committee answers to
the residents' association, and the hut is a walk from the streets it
serves.

## Who may join

An applicant must be eighteen or over on the day they apply and must live
at an address in one of the four postcodes the association covers: the
list is on the noticeboard and in the app under Help, and the committee
changes it only when the association's boundary changes. Proof of address
is a utility bill, a council letter, a bank statement, or a tenancy
agreement, dated within three months of the application and showing the
applicant's name and the address; a volunteer checks it against the
account at the desk and does not keep a copy. One membership per person:
an applicant who already has an account is told so and the application is
refused, and two members at one address are two memberships.

Toolshed does not ask for a reference, a deposit, or a credit check.
Applications are approved by any volunteer; declined applications are
decided by a committee member, who gives the reason in writing, and an
applicant who is declined may ask the committee to look again once.

## The subscription

The subscription is twenty pounds a year, due on approval and on each
anniversary, and is not refunded in part when a member leaves. A member
who cannot afford the subscription may ask the committee for a reduced
rate, which is decided in confidence; the service records only that a
reduced rate applies, not why. Volunteers who staff at least twelve desk
sessions in a membership year are not charged the following year's
subscription.

## Keeping a membership

A membership stays active for a year from approval and renews on payment
of the subscription. It ends, or is suspended, in these cases:

- A member who moves out of the four postcodes keeps their membership until
  its anniversary and is not renewed; they must return every tool before
  the anniversary.
- A member whose proof of address is found to have been false is suspended
  at once and the committee decides whether to end the membership.
- A member may be suspended for repeated late returns, for unreported
  damage, for lending a tool on to someone else, or for behaviour at the
  desk the volunteers will not accept; a suspension is for a stated period
  of up to six months, with the reason and the end date given in writing
  on the day.
- A member may end their membership at any time by telling the committee,
  once every tool is back and every charge paid.

## Lapsed members

A membership not renewed by its anniversary lapses. A lapsed member keeps
their account, their loan history, and their charges, and may renew at any
time to become active again; nothing is lost by lapsing except the right
to borrow and reserve. A tool out on loan when a membership lapses is late
from that day. The service keeps the account and its history for six years
after it is closed, which the association's record-keeping requires, and
deletes it after that unless a charge is still owed.
