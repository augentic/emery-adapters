# Reservation limits policy

Set by the committee; last changed 2026-01-20. Depends on:
[reservations](../reference/reservations.md).

A reservation is a promise to collect a tool, and a tool held for a member
who does not come is a tool nobody can use for a week. The limits here
keep queues honest: short enough that a place in one means something, and
with a cost to a member who takes a place and does not use it.

## Open reservations

A member may hold three open reservations at once, across any tools. An
open reservation is one that has not closed — not yet collected, missed,
cancelled, or withdrawn; a reservation that is `waiting` for collection is
still open and still counts. A member may not hold two reservations on the
same tool, and may not reserve a tool they currently have on loan. The
service refuses a fourth reservation and says that the limit has been
reached, so that the member can cancel one they no longer want.

## Missed collections

A missed collection is a hold that lapsed without being collected: a
reservation that became `waiting` and was not collected by the end of the
second desk session after the notice, a shelf hold not collected by the
end of the next desk session, or a `waiting` reservation the member
cancelled. Two shelf holds lapsing in one month count as one missed
collection; one `waiting` hold lapsing counts as one on its own. A
reservation withdrawn because the tool was retired is not a missed
collection.

A member who misses two collections in any three months is limited to one
open reservation until three months have passed without a miss. The
service tells the member when the reduced limit begins and when it ends,
and a reservation attempted under the reduced limit is refused with the
reason. Misses are counted from the day of the lapse; a dispute about a
miss — the member says they were not told the tool was waiting — goes to
the committee as any dispute does.

## How far ahead

A reservation can be made on a tool that is on loan however long its
queue; the estimate shown to the member is the service's best guess and
not a promise. A shelf hold can be made only for the next desk session. A
reservation cannot be made for a particular date in the future — the queue
is first come, first served — and a member who needs a tool on a certain
day borrows it the session before and, if it is a seven-day tool, extends
it.

## Holds and volunteers

A volunteer may collect a held tool on a member's behalf only when the
member has asked in writing from their account, and the collection is
recorded against both. A volunteer may not reserve a tool for a member who
has not asked, and may not move a member up a queue; a queue is changed
only by cancellation, lapse, or withdrawal.
