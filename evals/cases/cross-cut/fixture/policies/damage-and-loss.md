# Damage and loss policy

Set by the committee; last changed 2025-09-30. Depends on:
[catalogue](../reference/catalogue.md), [returns](../reference/returns.md).

Tools get damaged and tools get lost; the policy is about who pays and how
much, and it is written to reward honesty. A member who tells us a tool is
broken is treated more gently than one who hands it back and says nothing,
because the first saves the next member a wasted trip and perhaps an
injury.

## Reported damage

Damage reported in the app or at the desk before the tool is returned is
reported damage. For reported damage the member pays the cost of the
repair up to a cap of half the tool's replacement value, or nothing if the
volunteer who inspects the tool finds that it was wear rather than damage.
Where the tool cannot be repaired the member pays half its replacement
value. The committee may reduce or waive the charge where the damage was
plainly nobody's fault — a tool that failed through a hidden defect, a
blade that broke on a knot — and the member asks for that in the report.

From the moment damage is reported the tool is marked `damage reported` in
the catalogue, cannot be lent or reserved, and accrues no late charge for
that loan from that day; the member is asked to bring it back at the next
desk session.

## Unreported damage

Damage found by a volunteer at return that the member did not report is
unreported damage. For unreported damage the member pays the full cost of
the repair up to the tool's replacement value, or the full replacement
value where the tool cannot be repaired. There is no reduction for fault.
A second instance of unreported damage within twelve months is referred to
the committee and may lead to a suspension.

A case shortfall found at return that was not recorded when the loan
opened — a missing bit, blade, or charger — is unreported damage charged at
the part's value in the catalogue entry.

## Lost tools

A tool the member reports as lost, or a loan written off twenty-eight days
after its due date, is charged at the tool's replacement value in the
catalogue on the day of the write-off. A lost tool that is returned within
twenty-eight days of the report or the write-off has the replacement
charge refunded; a late charge waived at write-off is reinstated when the
tool comes back. A part lost from a case is charged at the part's value,
not the tool's.

Theft from a locked house or a locked car, with a police reference given
to the committee, is not charged. Theft from anywhere else — an unlocked
shed, a van overnight, a garden — is charged as a loss.

## Cleaning

A tool returned dirty enough that a volunteer must clean it before it can
go back on the shelf is charged a cleaning charge of five pounds, recorded
with the condition finding at return. Consumables supplied with a tool —
sanding sheets, blades, roller sleeves — are expected to be used up and
are never charged.

## Paying and disputing

Charges are added to the member's account with the receipt that explains
them and are paid at the desk in cash or by card, or in the app by card. A
charge over fifty pounds may be paid in up to three monthly parts by
arrangement with a volunteer. A member may dispute any charge within seven
days of the receipt by writing to the committee, which decides within a
month; a disputed charge is shown on the account but is not counted toward
the ten-pound limit that blocks borrowing until it is decided. Unpaid
charges over ten pounds block new loans; charges unpaid for three months
are referred to the committee.
