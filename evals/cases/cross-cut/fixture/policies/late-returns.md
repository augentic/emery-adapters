# Late returns policy

Set by the committee; last changed 2026-03-14. Depends on:
[returns](../reference/returns.md), [damage and loss](damage-and-loss.md).

Toolshed lends tools for a week because a week is long enough to finish
most jobs and short enough that four hundred tools serve a thousand
members. A tool kept past its due date is a tool another member cannot
have, so late returns are charged — not to make money, but to make the due
date mean something. The charges in this policy are the committee's and
change only by a committee decision minuted and recorded in the changelog.

## When a loan is late

A loan is late when its tool is returned after the due date. Lateness is
counted in whole days from the due date to the day of the return: a tool
due on a Tuesday and returned on the Wednesday is one day late. A tool
returned on its due date, at any session that day, is not late. The due
date is the one on the loan at the time of the return — the extended date
if the loan was extended — and a loan on a membership that lapsed or was
suspended during the loan is late from the day of the lapse or suspension
if that came first.

## The charge

The first day late is free. From the second day, the charge is one pound
per day for a seven-day tool and two pounds per day for a three-day tool,
for every day until the return, including days when the desk is closed.
The charge is added to the member's account when the tool is returned and
is shown on the return receipt. There is no cap on the late charge short of
the write-off below; a tool twenty days late at a pound a day owes nineteen
pounds.

A late charge is waived in full when the member reported damage on the
tool before it was due, for the days from the report onward, since a
damaged tool cannot be lent anyway and the member did the right thing in
saying so. A late charge is not waived for illness, holidays, or the desk
being closed on the day the member meant to come; a member who knows they
will be late extends the loan or asks a friend to bring the tool back.

## While a tool is overdue

A member with an overdue tool may not borrow another tool or make a
reservation until the overdue tool is back. Their existing reservations
stand, and a hold that becomes waiting while they are overdue runs on its
normal clock. The member is told on the due date that the tool is due, and
every seven days after that it is overdue, with the charge so far.

## Written off as lost

A tool twenty-eight days past its due date that has not been returned is
written off as lost. The tool's replacement value in the catalogue is
charged to the member's account, the late charge accrued is waived, the
loan is closed, and the tool is retired from the catalogue unless it comes
back. A tool returned within twenty-eight days of being written off
reverses this: the replacement charge is refunded, the late charge that
was waived is reinstated, and the tool goes back into the catalogue after
a volunteer has inspected it. A tool returned later than that is a
donation, and the replacement charge stands.

## Repeated lateness

Three late returns in any twelve months — counting every return more than
one day late — mean the committee writes to the member and may suspend the
membership for up to three months. Six in twelve months mean a suspension
of at least three months. A suspension for lateness is lifted early only
by a committee decision, and the member is told the reason and the end
date in writing on the day it is imposed.
