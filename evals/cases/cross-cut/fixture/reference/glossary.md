# Glossary

The words this documentation uses in a fixed sense. Where a word has a
state value in the service, the value is shown in code.

**Account.** The record the service keeps of one person: contact details,
membership, loans, reservations, charges, and sign-in credentials. One
person has one account.

**Anniversary.** The date a membership ends unless renewed: one year from
approval, or from the last renewal.

**Application.** A request to become a member, created with the account and
decided by a volunteer. States: waiting, approved, declined, lapsed.

**Availability.** What the catalogue says about whether a tool can be lent
right now: `on shelf`, `on loan`, `held`, or `unavailable`. Derived from
the tool's loans, holds, and condition; never set by hand.

**Case.** The box a tool is kept in, with the accessories that go with it.
The catalogue entry lists what should be in the case.

**Catalogue.** The list of every tool, with an entry per tool.

**Charge.** Money a member owes Toolshed: a late charge, a cleaning charge,
a damage charge, a replacement charge. Charges are added to the account
with the receipt that explains them and are paid at the desk or in the
app.

**Collection.** A member taking a tool that is held for them. A hold that
is not collected before it lapses is a missed collection.

**Committee.** The members elected to run Toolshed. Committee members can
approve applications, suspend and reinstate members, decide disputes, and
retire tools.

**Condition.** What the catalogue says about the state of a tool: `good`,
`worn`, `damage reported`, `under repair`, or `written off`.

**Desk.** The counter in the hut where volunteers lend and take back
tools, during a desk session.

**Desk session.** A period when the desk is open: Tuesday and Thursday
evenings, Saturday mornings. Several rules count time in desk sessions
rather than days.

**Dispute.** A member's objection to a condition finding or a charge,
raised within seven days of the receipt and decided by the committee.

**Due date.** The last day a tool may be returned without being late.

**Entry.** One tool's record in the catalogue.

**Extension.** Moving a loan's due date later, once, by one to seven days.

**Fair use.** The committee's policy on how many tools a member may have
out at once.

**Hold.** A tool kept at the desk for a particular member: a shelf hold
made on an available tool, or a reservation that has reached the head of
its queue. A held tool's availability is `held`.

**Label.** The number printed on a tool and encoded in its barcode, which
identifies its catalogue entry.

**Lapse.** A membership that has passed its anniversary unrenewed, an
application undecided for thirty days, or a hold that was not collected.

**Late.** A loan whose tool is scanned in after its due date. Counted in
whole days from the due date.

**Loan.** One tool lent to one member, from the volunteer's confirmation at
the desk to the scan that returns it.

**Loan limit.** The number of tools a member may have out at once, set in
the fair-use policy.

**Loan period.** How long a tool is lent for: seven days, or three for a
high-demand tool.

**Lost.** A tool that cannot be found, whether reported by the member or
written off after twenty-eight days overdue. Charged at replacement value.

**Member.** A person with an `active` membership.

**Membership.** An account's standing with Toolshed: `applied`, `active`,
`lapsed`, or `suspended`.

**Membership card.** The card with a member's number, scanned at the desk.

**Notification.** A message the service sends a member or volunteer about
something that has happened or is about to.

**Queue.** The reservations on one tool, in the order they were made.

**Receipt.** The message sent when a loan opens, is extended, or is
returned, carrying the dates, the condition, and any charge.

**Replacement value.** What it would cost to buy a tool again, set in the
catalogue and reviewed yearly; what is charged for a lost tool.

**Reservation.** A member's place in the queue for a tool. States: open,
`waiting`, `collected`, `missed`, `cancelled`, `withdrawn`.

**Return.** The scan at the desk that closes a loan.

**Shelf hold.** A reservation made on a tool that is on the shelf, holding
it until the end of the next desk session.

**Suspension.** A committee member's decision that a member may not borrow
or reserve until a date no more than six months away.

**Unreported damage.** Damage found at return that the member did not
report during the loan. Charged at a higher rate than reported damage.

**Volunteer.** A member who staffs the desk. Volunteers act from their own
account, and what they do for a member is recorded against both.

**Wear.** A tool failing from age or ordinary use. Not charged.

**Written off.** A tool the committee has decided will not be repaired, or
a loan whose tool is twenty-eight days overdue and treated as lost.

## Words we avoid

A few words are not used in these documents because they have meant
different things to different volunteers.

**Booking.** Members sometimes say they have "booked" a tool. The service
has reservations and holds, and a reservation is a place in a queue, not
a date; "booking" suggested a date and led to disappointment.

**Fine.** The charges for late returns, damage, and cleaning are charges,
not fines. The committee is not a court, and the word made volunteers
reluctant to apply the policy.

**Renting.** Toolshed lends; it does not rent. Members pay a subscription
and the charges in the policies, and nothing per loan.

**Overdue** and **late** are used in these documents in one sense: a loan
is late when its tool comes back after the due date, and overdue while it
is out past the due date. The two words describe the same loan from the
return's side and from the shelf's.
