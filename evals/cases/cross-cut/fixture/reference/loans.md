# Loans

Depends on: [accounts](accounts.md), [catalogue](catalogue.md),
[fair use](../policies/fair-use.md), [returns](returns.md).

A loan is one tool lent to one member from the moment a volunteer confirms
it at the desk until the moment a volunteer scans the tool back in. Every
loan refers to one catalogue entry and one account and carries its start,
its due date, its return if it has one, the condition recorded at each end,
and the charges that arose from it.

## Opening a loan

A loan opens when a volunteer scans a tool and a membership card and
confirms the due date. Before the loan opens the service checks, in this
order, that the card is current and the membership `active`; that the
tool's availability is `on shelf`, or `held` for this member; that the
member has room under the loan limit in the fair-use policy; that the
member has no overdue loan; and that the member's unpaid charges do not
exceed ten pounds. The first check to fail refuses the loan with its own
message, and the volunteer sees which check failed. A tool `held` for
another member, or reserved by one, is never lent to a walk-in, whatever
the shelf says.

The due date proposed is the start date plus the tool's loan period —
seven days, or three for a tool the catalogue marks as such — and the
volunteer may shorten it at the member's request but not lengthen it. The
contents of the case are checked against the catalogue entry and any
shortfall is recorded against the previous loan before this one opens.
A receipt naming the tool, the start, and the due date is sent to the
member when the loan opens.

## Extending a loan

A loan may be extended once, by one to seven days, as long as the tool has
no reservation and the loan is not overdue. Extension replaces the due
date, sends a new receipt, and marks the loan extended; a second extension
is refused, as is an extension of a reserved or overdue loan, each with a
message naming the reason. A member may extend from the app or ask a
volunteer at the desk.

## Loans and the member's standing

While a member has an overdue loan they may not open another loan or make
a reservation. The overdue loan is counted from the day after the due date
and the counting, the charges, and the lost-tool rule are the returns
reference's and the late returns policy's. A loan on a membership that
lapses or is suspended is treated as overdue from the day of the lapse or
suspension, and the member is told so with the notice of lapse or
suspension.

## Closing a loan

A loan closes when a volunteer scans the tool back in — see the returns
reference for what is recorded and charged. A loan also closes when its
tool is written off as lost under the damage and loss policy, with the
replacement charge recorded against it; the loan reopens as returned if
the tool is brought back within the refund window.

## History

A member's loans are kept on the account and shown on the account page,
most recent first, with the tool, the dates, the condition findings, and
the charges. A volunteer may see any member's history at the desk; a
member sees only their own. Loan history is kept for six years after the
account closes.
