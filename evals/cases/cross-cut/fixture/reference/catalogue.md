# Catalogue

Depends on: [damage and loss](../policies/damage-and-loss.md).

The catalogue is the list of every tool Toolshed owns, has owned, or has
been offered, with what the service knows about each. Members browse it to
find a tool, volunteers maintain it at the desk, and every loan,
reservation, and condition finding refers to an entry in it.

## Entries

Each tool has one entry, identified by a label number printed on the tool
and encoded in the barcode on it. An entry carries the tool's name, its
kind (one of a fixed list the committee maintains: drills, saws, sanders,
garden, ladders, decorating, plumbing, measuring, and so on), its make and
model where known, a photograph, a short description of what it is for,
the list of what comes in its case, its replacement value, and the loan
period that applies to it — seven days unless the entry says three. An
entry also carries the tool's condition and its availability, which are
described below. Two tools of the same make and model are two entries,
each with its own label; a member reserves a particular tool, not a kind.

Replacement value is what it would cost Toolshed to buy the tool, or an
equivalent, today, and is reviewed once a year by the committee; the value
in force when a tool is lost or written off is the value charged. Parts in
a case have their own values, listed with the case contents, so that a
lost bit is charged as a bit and not as a drill.

## Condition

A tool's condition is one of `good`, `worn`, `damage reported`, `under
repair`, and `written off`. A new tool is `good`; a volunteer may mark a
working tool `worn` when it shows its age so that members know what to
expect. A damage report moves the tool to `damage reported` until a
volunteer has inspected it, after which it goes to `good` or `worn` if it
works, to `under repair` if it will be fixed, or to `written off` if it
will not. A tool `under repair` or `written off` cannot be lent or
reserved, and a tool written off is retired from the catalogue — its entry
stays, marked retired, so that past loans still refer to it, but it does
not appear in searches.

## Availability

Availability is derived, not set: a tool is `on shelf` when no loan is open
on it and no hold or reservation is waiting for collection, `on loan` when
a loan is open, `held` when a shelf hold or a reservation is waiting for a
member to collect it, and `unavailable` when its condition prevents
lending. A tool `on loan` shows the date it is due back and whether it has
been reserved; it never shows who has it.

## Searching and browsing

Members search by name, by kind, and by whether the tool is available now,
and sort by name or by the date it is due back. Retired tools are left out
of every search and every listing unless a volunteer asks for them.
Catalogue pages are public — anyone with the link can see a tool's page
without signing in — but reserving from a page needs an active membership.

## Donations

A tool offered to Toolshed is entered as a `proposed` entry with the
donor's name, and a volunteer inspects it before it is accepted; an entry
that is accepted becomes a tool like any other, and one declined is
removed from the catalogue after the donor has been told. Toolshed does
not accept petrol tools, chainsaws, or anything the insurer excludes, and
the catalogue refuses an entry of those kinds at the point it is proposed.
