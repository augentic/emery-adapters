# Getting started

Depends on: [membership eligibility](../policies/membership-eligibility.md),
[accounts](../reference/accounts.md).

## Joining Toolshed

Membership is open to adults who live in the Hollow Lane area; the exact
rules are the committee's and are written in the membership eligibility
policy. To join, you create an account in the app or at the desk, give your
name, your address, and an email address or mobile number we can reach you
on, and pay the annual subscription. A volunteer checks your proof of
address — a utility bill, a council letter, or a tenancy agreement dated
within the last three months — and approves the application, usually at
the next desk session. Until the application is approved you can browse
the catalogue and see what is in and what is out, but you cannot borrow or
reserve, and the app tells you that your application is waiting rather
than letting you try.

Your membership runs for twelve months from the day it is approved. About
a month before it ends we send a reminder; if you renew before the
anniversary your membership simply continues, and if you let it lapse your
account stays but becomes a browsing account again until you renew. A
lapsed member who still has a tool out is treated as late from the day the
membership lapsed, whatever the loan's due date said.

## Your first loan

Once approved, you can borrow straight away. Find the tool in the catalogue
— every tool has a page with its photograph, its condition, what comes in
the case with it, and whether it is on the shelf or out — and either take
it from the shelf at the desk or reserve it for a later session if it is
out. A volunteer scans the tool and your membership card, confirms the due
date with you, and the loan is yours. New members may have one tool out at
a time for their first month; after that the usual limit in the fair-use
policy applies.

## What you are agreeing to

By joining you agree to return tools on time, clean and complete, to the
desk and to a volunteer — never left outside the hut — and to pay the
charges set out in the policies when you do not. You also agree that the
committee may suspend a membership for repeated late returns, for damage
that is not reported, or for lending a borrowed tool to someone else, and
that a suspension is told to you in writing with the reason and the date
it ends.

## Getting help

Volunteers at the desk can do anything the app can do, and a few things it
cannot. If you cannot get to a desk session, the committee's email address
is on the noticeboard and in the app under Help, and a volunteer answers
within three days.
