# Reserving a tool

Depends on: [reservations](../reference/reservations.md),
[reservation limits](../policies/reservation-limits.md).

## When a tool is out

If the tool you want is on loan, its catalogue page shows the date it is
due back and a Reserve button. Reserving puts you in the queue for the
tool: when it is returned, the first member in the queue is told that it
is waiting for them at the desk, and the tool is held for them until the
end of the second desk session after the notice — about a week — before it
goes to the next in the queue or back to the shelf. A tool with a
reservation is never lent from the shelf to someone who walks in, and a
loan of a reserved tool cannot be extended, so the queue moves at the pace
of the due dates.

You can see your place in the queue on the tool's page and on your account
page, and the app estimates when your turn will come from the due dates of
the loan and the reservations ahead of you. The estimate is an estimate:
an early return brings your turn forward, and a late one pushes it back.

## Reserving a tool that is on the shelf

You can also reserve a tool that is on the shelf, which holds it for you
until the end of the next desk session. This is for members who cannot get
to the desk on the day and do not want to find the tool gone; a tool held
this way is marked "held" on its page and a volunteer will not lend it to
anyone else. A shelf hold that is not collected lapses at the end of the
session and the tool goes back to being available. Two shelf holds that
lapse in a month count as one missed collection under the reservation
limits policy.

## Cancelling and limits

Cancel a reservation from your account page at any time before you are
told the tool is waiting; a reservation cancelled after that counts as a
missed collection. A member may hold three reservations at once, across
any tools, and a member who misses two collections in three months is
limited to one until three months have passed without a miss. The limits
are set by the committee in the reservation limits policy, and the app
enforces them when you try to reserve: it tells you which limit you have
reached rather than quietly refusing.

## A tool you reserved has been withdrawn

Sometimes a tool is withdrawn while you are in the queue — it has been
damaged beyond repair, or lost, or the committee has retired it. Everyone
in the queue is told, the reservations are cancelled without counting
against anyone, and if the catalogue has another tool of the same kind the
notice names it so you can reserve that instead.
