# Returning a tool

Depends on: [returns](../reference/returns.md),
[late returns](../policies/late-returns.md).

## At the desk

Bring the tool back to the desk during a session and hand it to a
volunteer. The volunteer scans the label, checks the tool over and the
contents of its case against the catalogue page, and marks the loan
returned; the app sends you a receipt at once with the date and time and
the condition recorded. The return is complete when the volunteer scans
the tool, and not before — a tool handed to another member, left on the
shelf, or left outside the hut is still on loan to you and is still your
responsibility, and if it goes missing between your leaving it and a
volunteer finding it, the loss is yours under the damage and loss policy.

If the volunteer finds damage or something missing from the case that was
not recorded when the loan started, they record it with you there and then
and the charge, if any, follows the damage and loss policy. You can dispute
a condition finding within seven days by writing to the committee; until
the dispute is settled the charge is shown on your account but is not
counted against your right to borrow.

## Returning late

A tool is late from the day after its due date. The app tells you on the
due date that the tool is due, and again each week it stays out. A late
return is charged by the day at the rate in the late returns policy, with
the first day free so that a tool returned the day after it was due costs
nothing; the charge is added to your account when the tool is scanned back
in, and shows on the receipt. While you have a tool overdue you cannot
borrow or reserve another, and a tool that is still out four weeks after
its due date is treated as lost and charged at the catalogue's replacement
value, with the late charge waived. Returning a lost tool within a further
four weeks has the replacement charge refunded, less the late charge that
was waived. Three late returns in any twelve months mean a conversation
with the committee and may mean a suspension.

## Returning early and returning dirty

You can return a tool any time before it is due, and an early return of a
tool with a reservation is a kindness to the next member, who is told at
once. A tool should come back as clean as it went out; a tool returned
dirty — paint on the roller tray, mud in the pressure washer hose, sawdust
packed into the sander — is accepted, but the volunteer records it and the
cleaning charge in the damage and loss policy is added to your account.
Consumables that come with a tool, such as sanding sheets and blades, are
expected to be used, and nothing is charged for them.
