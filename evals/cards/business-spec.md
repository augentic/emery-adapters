# The business-specification round

A round of the eval, written and read under [the protocol](README.md#protocol); [the README](README.md#rounds) indexes every round.

The specification is meant to read as a behaviour-driven business document — what the system does for those it serves, and what they observe when it cannot — and the design as how the program readies itself and reaches other systems. In revision `072acf48…` of `train` (the live typescript example, 2026-10-15, no card) 23 of 45 requirements were `start.*` and `key-vault.*`: the Confluent secret read from Key Vault and watched for rotation, APM initialised, the consumer and producer constructed with SSL and SASL, the stops prefetched with retry and refreshed hourly, the token retriever provided, the status check and the 60 s status log, the stop, and the exit timers; `plan.json` made four of its six slices from them; and the design turn had already labelled `KeyVault`, `ConfluentSecretRetriever`, `GtfsApi`, `BlockMgtApi`, `Config`, and `Main` `(mechanism)`, so the specification contradicted the design's own classification. The pollution enters at extraction and cannot be fixed later: the engine copies the winning claim's statement verbatim into the requirement's body, the invariant kept (the model never authors a document). Three seams feed it, and the arm changes each:

- **The SDK's survey** (emery `RELEASES.md` 0.38.0, "The specification states what the system does for those it serves"): the facts no longer list the modules only the bootstrap reaches, so an upstream client or a secret retriever is a mechanism reached through the surface or bootstrap that uses it and never a surface; `Surface::start`'s brief note says the bootstrap is claimed for what those the program serves observe of it as a whole and that how it readies itself, reaches other systems, and shuts down is the design's; the decision-points section names a line that constructs a client or a connection as no requirement's anchor; the calls section says every listed call that leaves the process is a `call`, with a `requirement` beside it only where what the caller observes depends on it; and `claims.md`'s "Behaviour, not technology" is rewritten around the one rule, with the helper rule and the vocabulary rule beside it.
- **The adapters' prose** (this tree's `RELEASES.md` 0.13.0, "`typescript` and `python` extract the specification as what the system does for those it serves"): `survey.md` names no service surface; `extract.md`'s `start` boundary, error-plumbing rule, sequencing rule, helper rule, vocabulary test, stated-behaviour lead, and the `Calls` and `Excerpts` rows that keep a dropped requirement's `call` and `excerpt`.
- **The engine's synthesis prose** (emery 0.38.0): `requirement-block.md` says the body is the winning claim's verbatim and the scenarios use the domain's words, never the code's; `design-format.md`'s `apis` and `technical-logic` sections and `claim-landing.md`'s `excerpt` and `call` rows have the design hold every outbound endpoint with its fields, timeout, retry rule, and refresh interval, and every readiness and lifecycle step, so nothing the specification drops is lost; `claim-landing.md` joins the design turn's prose.

The runner gains the measure recall lacks: `[[absent]]` spans in `expected.toml`, each hit by an accepted `requirement` claim held to it, printed `absent hits: N` per run with the span and the claim and as a band under `Stability`; and `mechanism` now counts a body naming a design type whose role is `mechanism`, since the design's `data` types alone are the program's vocabulary. `train`'s expectation moves with the rule — `stems = ["train-update"]`, the `start` surface kept and `key-vault` dropped, 14 `train-update` requirements and six criteria kept, the two Confluent criteria dropped with their requirements, seven `[[absent]]` spans — and `train-pair`, `express-orders`, and `fastapi-routers` mirror it, each move recorded in the case's header comment. The components and the release `emery` are built from the two working trees; the predictions and the rule were written before any run; the runs are the operator's.

## Cases

Treated: `train` and `train-pair`, the two whose expectations moved for the rule. Controls: `express-orders` and `fastapi-routers`, whose `start` items moved to `[[absent]]` and whose middleware, CORS, error mapping, required-config, and health items stay; `nestjs-boilerplate`, `django-shop`, and `django-styleguide`, where the retired facts section laid (wiring alone, [the service modules](sdk-rules.md#the-service-modules)) and nothing moves.

First, with no turn spent: `cargo run -p evals -- --facts train train-pair`, read for the section gone from each case's `facts.md`. The `start` note's new sentence reaches no artifact of the runner, since a `--facts` run ends at the survey turn before any brief is laid; `tests/source.rs` shows it with no turn spent either, in `typescript`, `python`, and the two `*_bootstrap_clients` scenarios. Then three specify-only runs a case under `CURSOR_MODEL` pinned to `composer-2.5`, `EVAL_RUNS=3`: `cargo run -p evals -- train train-pair express-orders fastapi-routers nestjs-boilerplate django-shop django-styleguide`.

## Decision rule

- **The arm holds** when `train` and `train-pair` meet P1, P2, P3, and P7 and the controls meet P8's surfaces, stems, and `absent` clauses; the three rules then ship as built, and P4 and P8's recall are read as bands for the next card.
- **A family's passage fails.** `absent hits` ≥ 2 on ≥ 2/3 runs from one anchor family — credentials (`start.ts`, `confluent-secret-retriever.ts`, `key-vault.ts`), prefetch (`gtfs-api.ts`), lifecycle (`main.ts#L34-L67`, `#L147-L161`, `start.ts`'s exit handlers), ack (`main.ts#L36-L45`) — is that family's sentence in `extract.md`'s `start` boundary, named with the hits, amended, and rerun; hits spread one to a family on every run are the rule's weight against the model's habit and the SDK's `claims.md` is the seam, reread before any prose moves.
- **The design loses what the specification dropped.** A miss on P6's hand-read list is Part 2's `technical-logic` or `apis` wording in `design-format.md`, the item named and rerun before shipping; the specification is not the place to put it back.
- **The plan still cuts `start`.** P3 failing with P1 holding — a `start` slice over zero requirements — is the engine's slicing, not the arm's, and recorded against the engine.
- **A control moves.** A listed wiring module named as a surface on ≥ 2/3 runs of a control is the survey prompt's "Naming a module" paragraph, as the service-modules round's rule read it; `express-orders`' `start` losing its middleware or required-config items on ≥ 2/3 runs is the `start` boundary over-reaching — "a rule applied to every request" and "a setting it refuses to run without" made the lead — amended and rerun.
- **Recall decides nothing** alone, as every round holds; a run the backend refuses before any claim is put again once; a run that fails in the engine's turns is graded on its claims.
