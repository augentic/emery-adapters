## The shared rules

The adapters adopt the SDK's two shared rules (engine `emery-sdk` 0.38 at `33e3665a`, [RELEASES.md](../../RELEASES.md)), giving them what only an adapter can. The first rule is the bound step: a call into the tree whose result is bound (`users = user_list(..)`, `const total = repo.total(id)`) is a `requirement`'s anchor as an awaited call or one made for its effect alone is, unless it constructs — each walker records the SDK's `Use::Bound` for the call an assignment, a declaration, or a `with` item binds, and `python`'s walker now binds `with` targets from their context expressions, so `cur.execute(..)` on a cursor a pool's connection opened traces to the pool's package. The second is the widening's scope: a seam whose modules import what the resolver cannot follow, or load a module by a computed name, lays after its closure the rest of the importing module's package, or of the package the load's literal head spells (`f"app.plugins.{m.name}"`, `plugins.__path__`, `` `./plugins/${name}` ``), in place of the rest of the tree; and an import reaching a test module the keep set aside is `Target::Skipped`, neither unresolved nor followed. The facts the survey turn lays read almost none of this — `--facts` over the sixteen cases against the PR 7 components (the committed tree built `--locked`, its `emery-sdk` at `7ab7abe0`) is byte-identical on fifteen, and `django-styleguide`'s differs by one clause: its unfollowed-imports sentence no longer says `styleguide_example.utils.tests` from `common/factories.py` "names no module of the tree", since the resolver now answers `Skipped` for a test package the keep set aside, and the computed load at `api/mixins.py#L43` is all it names — so the round asks what the two rules do to the extraction turns: the anchors they admit, the files they lay. One graded round per adapter, operator-invoked, three runs under `CURSOR_MODEL` pinned to `composer-2.5`, over the components built from this tree once `make ci` is green; `python` read against `2026-10-01T22-58-28Z` with `2026-10-02T02-37-57Z` and `2026-10-01T22-30-32Z` for `django-styleguide`, `typescript` against `2026-09-29T22-28-34Z` and the cards since.

## The service modules

The first live `omnia` build of a real tree (`r9k`, 2026-10-13, no card) was handed a `start` slice of 25 of the plan's 45 requirements: the GTFS client, the block-management client, the Confluent secret retriever with its cache, APM initialisation, the listener, and the status routes, since the bootstrap — a `Main` class — alone reached the modules that hold them, none of them registers or decorates anything, and `Surface::start`'s closure stops at the named surfaces' entries and nowhere else. The survey had no name for a module that talks to a system outside the process, so `start` swallowed each whole, and the build then wrote 1,655 lines of lifecycle simulation under `crates/start/` against the target's guardrails. The arm is SDK-owned with adapter prose (emery `RELEASES.md` 0.38.0, "The survey's facts list the service modules"; this tree's `RELEASES.md` 0.13.0, "`typescript` and `python` have the survey name the service modules"): the facts gain a section of the modules only the bootstrap reaches — in the bootstrap's closure stopping at the modules that locate a surface, reached by no such module, nothing in them registered or decorated, a package imported — each with what it exports and the packages; `survey.md` for both languages names a service module whose export is a service of its own — a system reached, a protocol spoken — as a surface at the export under the export's name kebab-cased whole (`GtfsApi` → `gtfs-api`), and leaves settings and a client constructed and handed on to `start`; `derive` reads the anchor as an exported class or function already, an id per public method.

The facts were read first, with no turn spent, as [the adapters as recognisers](#the-adapters-as-recognisers) read theirs: `cargo run -p evals -- --facts typescript python` over the components built from the engine working tree and a release `emery` built beside them, 22 cases. The section's first cut — every module in the bootstrap's whole closure that imports a package — laid on ten cases and was wrong on sight: `nestjs-boilerplate` listed 58 modules, every service, DTO, repository, and guard a controller reaches, `cli-jobs` its `config.ts`, `db.ts`, `queues.ts`, and the CSV parser, which its command modules reach, `django-shop` its views and services. A module a surface's module reaches is that surface's already, and listing it invites the model to name it twice, so the cut became the closure stopping at the locating modules less what those modules reach, which is the `start` closure the SDK will cut once the surfaces are named. Under it the section lays on **four of 22 cases**, each listing the wiring the bootstrap alone holds and no client: `express-orders` the error middleware (`src/middleware/errors.ts`, `notFound` and `errorHandler` through `zod`); `nestjs-boilerplate`, and so `nestjs-pair`'s code source, two utilities (`validation-options.ts`, `serializer.interceptor.ts`); `django-shop` the settings, the URL conf, the WSGI entry, the app config, and the signal receiver (`orders/signals.py`, `order_saved`); `django-styleguide` the settings modules, three URL confs, the WSGI entry, the logging and debug-toolbar setup, and the Celery app (`tasks/celery.py`). On the eighteen others — `cli-jobs`, `click-jobs`, `fastify-demo`, `cli-table`, `flask-blueprints`, the three `fastapi-*`, `hono-mounted`, `api-routes`, `money-library`, the `orders-*` set — it lays nothing: every client and store those trees hold is reached by a handler or a command module, so no module of the plan's candidate cases (`cli-jobs`, `click-jobs`, `fastify-demo`, `nestjs-boilerplate`, `nestjs-pair`) leaves `start`, and **no `expected.toml` moves**. The case the rule was written for is `r9k`, retired from the set before this round, and no authored case has its shape — a bootstrap constructing clients and handing them to handlers as values — which the recommendation below takes up. `orders-triad` and `nestjs-pair` lay no code-source facts under `--facts`: the inline `intent` brief's or the `documentation` source's first turn fails before the code source's survey logs, a runner race the two multi-source cases share and no reading of the arm.

### Predictions

- **The four cases.** Surfaces and stems exact against their bands on 3/3 runs — `express-orders` `4/4` and 28–30/30, `nestjs-boilerplate` `10/10 +2` and 39–40/42, `django-shop` `4/4` and 14–17/17, `django-styleguide` `13/13` and 56–60/61 — with the listed modules named as surfaces by **no run**: each is settings, wiring, or a hook, which the section's own sentence and the prompt's grain rule leave to `start`. The survey turn one attempt on ≥ 2/3 runs; no run climbs the ladder.
- **The eighteen others.** Byte-identical facts but for nothing — the section is absent — so every figure as its card.
- **A case of `r9k`'s shape**, once authored (below): the client modules listed, each named at its export under the kebab of the export's name on ≥ 2/3 runs, with an id per public method in the brief, and the requirements at their calls through the package landing under those stems rather than `start`; `start` holding the bootstrap's own lines alone.

### Decision rule

- **The rule holds** when the four cases read their bands with no listed module named as a surface on ≥ 2/3 runs each, and the eighteen read as their cards. The section then stays as cut, and the authored case decides what it was built for.
- **The section over-reaches** when a listed wiring module is named on ≥ 2/3 runs of a case: the prompt's sentence is read as an invitation, and the fix is the section's wording — "leave what only reads settings … to `start`" made the lead rather than the tail — recorded here and rerun.
- **Recall decides nothing** alone, as every round holds.

### Results

Not run past the facts, and **superseded** by [the business-specification round](business-spec.md) before the graded runs. The arm was built on a reading the round reverses: it took the `start` slice's 25 requirements for a survey with no name for a module that talks to a system outside the process, and named the clients as surfaces so their requirements would land elsewhere. The requirements were the problem, not their stem — a credential loaded, a table prefetched, a client constructed, a status log are how the process readies itself, the design's in `excerpt` and `call` claims and no requirement's, under the one rule emery 0.38.0's `claims.md` now states — so the facts section is retired (the SDK lays no "Modules only the bootstrap reaches"), `survey.md` names no service surface, and a client or a retriever is a mechanism reached through the surface or bootstrap that uses it, in `start`'s closure and the design. The four cases' bands stand as this section's predictions wrote them and are read as controls there. The recommendation is taken up in the opposite sense: the case in `r9k`'s shape is `train` itself, vendored since, and its expectation now holds the clients as `[[absent]]` spans rather than surfaces; `tests/source.rs`'s `emery_typescript_bootstrap_clients` and `emery_python_bootstrap_clients` are that shape in miniature, asserting the facts offer neither client and both sit in `start`'s closure.
