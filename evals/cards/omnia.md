## The omnia target's first card

The second target adapter, [`omnia`](../../targets/omnia/), lands a plan as an omnia 0.36.0 HTTP guest in Rust: a root `Cargo.toml` holding the workspace and every pin, a root crate `app/` holding the `wasip3` export and the router list, neither the workspace manifest nor the export's file edited by any slice once laid; one crate per stem under `crates/<stem>/` (`app-<stem>`), every slice a `src/<part>.rs` listed by a `pub mod` line; `#[tokio::test]` tests per scenario under the stem's `tests/`; four lists slices append to and nothing else edited — the root crate's `[dependencies]` in `app/Cargo.toml`, the router list `app/src/routes.rs`, a stem's `pub mod` list in `crates/<stem>/src/lib.rs`, a stem's `[dependencies]` — each a union-merged file holding one block slices add lines to; `Cargo.lock` kept `ours`, and everything else, the pins, the export, and the six root files laid byte for byte among it, unruled. Its slice loop is `cargo clippy -p app-<stem> --all-targets -- -D warnings`, `cargo test -p app-<stem>`, `cargo check --target wasm32-wasip2`; its verify holds the six root files to the layout, then `cargo clippy --workspace`, `cargo test --workspace`, `cargo build --target wasm32-wasip2`, and a `find -newer` over the tree for what the checks wrote, since the rerun's reading has it that no repository is reachable from `$WORKSPACE`. Nothing of it has met the model: the prose was written from a spike laid by hand, whose figures are the only ones this section has — cold on this machine, `clippy` 43 s, `cargo test` 22 s more, the wasm32 check 9 s, about 75 s a pass, a verify's three commands about 90 s, a cold registry fetch of some 600 MB before the first, and 2.4 GB of `target/` per working copy.

### Decision rule

- **Read against the rerun's row.** The plan, the binary, the backend, the boundary, and the model are that row's, so a failure this card shows and that row does not is the target's — its prose, its layout, its rules, or the Rust toolchain under the fence — and never the boundary's; a failure both show is the boundary's and recorded there.
